# Dailies 1.10.0

Friends. It installs over 1.9.1 and keeps everything, every setting included. If you're on 1.9.1, you can install it from **Settings → About → Check now**.

## Friends (Settings → Friends)
- **Sign in with your email.** Dailies emails you a 6-digit code; type it in and you're signed in. No password. The first time makes your account.
- **One account, app and web.** Sign in with the same email in the Android app and the web version and you're the same player in both.
- **Display name**: what your friends see ("Player" until you change it). Your email is never shown to anyone.
- **Your friend code** (like VT98-VDJX), with Copy and Share. Friends add you by typing it in.
- **Add a friend** by their code. They get a request in Settings → Friends to accept or decline (and the Settings list shows "1 friend request waiting"). If they had already asked you, you're friends straight away.
- **Friends, requests and sent requests**, with Remove, Accept/Decline and Cancel.
- **Sign out**, and **Delete account** (your name, friend code and friends are deleted; it can't be undone).
- Your games, logs and streaks stay on your device as before: nothing about them is sent yet. Leaderboards come in a later version.

## Requirements
- Android 8.0 or newer. Friends needs an internet connection.
